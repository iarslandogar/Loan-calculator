It is client-only, with no network calls after load.

  • v1 excludes: S-14 to S-19 (comparing offers, a maximum loan from a budget, copy, export, prepayments, yearly summaries). It also excludes fees, flat-rate or variable loans, payment dates, currency conversion, accounts and other languages.

  • interest = the opening balance × r, rounded half-up to 2 dp;
  • the payment is recomputed as the EMI of the remaining balance over the remaining months, then rounded;
  • principal = payment − interest;
  • the last month pays off whatever balance is left.

The headline figure is month 1's payment. Every month is within 0.01 of it, and the balance ends at exactly 0.00. The textbook "fixed payment, last one adjusts" method is ruled out because it fails within the accepted inputs.

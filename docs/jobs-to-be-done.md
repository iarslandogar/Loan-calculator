# Jobs To Be Done

A job describes what the person needs to get done whether or not this app exists. No job mentions the app, a screen or a control. Each job belongs to exactly one role (see `app-roles.md`), and each job is served by one or more stories (see `user-stories.md`).

## R-1 Prospective Borrower

**J-1 (Prospective Borrower).** "When I am offered a loan for something I want to buy, I want to know what I would have to pay each month, so I can decide whether it fits my budget before I commit."

**J-2 (Prospective Borrower).** "When I am weighing loan offers with different rates or durations, I want to understand the full cost of borrowing, so I can choose the offer that costs me least overall."

**J-3 (Prospective Borrower).** "When a lender quotes me figures in a particular currency, I want to see amounts in that currency's familiar notation, so I can relate them to my own money without misreading them."

**J-10 (Prospective Borrower).** "When I am checking figures late in the evening or in a dim room, I want to read them without glare or eye strain, so I can concentrate on the numbers rather than the screen."

**J-11 (Prospective Borrower).** "When I am away from my desk, at a showroom or a bank branch, I want to work out repayments on whatever device I have with me, so I can respond to an offer on the spot."

## R-2 Loan Advisor

**J-4 (Loan Advisor).** "When a customer asks me for a repayment quote during a meeting, I want to produce accurate figures quickly, so I can answer on the spot and keep the conversation moving."

**J-5 (Loan Advisor).** "When a customer questions how a figure was reached, I want to show how each payment splits between interest and repayment of the debt, so I can justify the quote and keep their trust."

**J-6 (Loan Advisor).** "When I have quoted a customer several options earlier in the day, I want to bring back a previous quote exactly, so I can compare or repeat it without re-entering the details."

**J-7 (Loan Advisor).** "When I finish with one customer and start with the next, I want to remove the previous customer's figures, so I can protect their privacy."

## R-3 Existing Borrower

**J-8 (Existing Borrower).** "When I am partway through repaying a loan, I want to know how much I will still owe after a given number of payments, so I can plan an early repayment or a refinance."

**J-9 (Existing Borrower).** "When I look back at what I have repaid, I want to understand how much of my money went to interest rather than reducing the debt, so I can judge whether paying extra would be worthwhile."
